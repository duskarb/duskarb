## About

I am an Industrial Design student at **KAIST** working across **interactive media, physical computing, web experiments, and research-driven design**.

I am interested in what happens when familiar technologies become slightly unfamiliar. My work often starts with something playful or ordinary—a button, an interface, a document, a tool—and turns it into a situation where people can notice their own **attention, expectations, trust, hesitation, and behavior**.

Rather than treating technology as a way to make things simply faster or easier, I use it as a **stage for human experience**.

> I make things that make people stop and think twice.

---

## What I'm exploring

**Human × Non-human**  
How interfaces, algorithms, sensors, and physical mechanisms shape human behavior.

**Playful Criticality**  
Using familiar and humorous forms to introduce uncomfortable or unexpected questions.

**Interaction as Material**  
Treating waiting, attention, feedback, errors, and gestures as design materials—not just outcomes.

**Technology as a Stage**  
Building situations where invisible systems become physically or experientially noticeable.

## A little more

I like ideas that begin with a question rather than an answer.

I am especially drawn to the space between **design and art, physical and digital, human and machine, useful and useless**.

Sometimes that means building an interface. Sometimes it means making a weird object. Sometimes it means designing a situation that shouldn't exist in the first place.

That is usually where things get interesting.
